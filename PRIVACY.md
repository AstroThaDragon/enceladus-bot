Privacy Policy for Enceladus
Last Updated: September 19, 2026
Enceladus is a Discord bot operated for use within The Cosmic Lair. This Privacy Policy explains what information Enceladus may access, what information it stores for its features, how that information is used, and how it may be handled by the services used to operate the bot.


1. Information Enceladus May Access
To provide its features, Enceladus may receive or process information made available through the Discord API, including:
- Discord User Information: Information such as a user's Discord user ID, username, display name, server membership, roles, and other basic member information needed for bot features.
- Message Content: Message content may be processed when required for a specific command or feature, including the !qr (Quick Report) system.
- Server and Member Events: Enceladus may process events such as members joining or leaving the server, server boosts, member updates, and other Discord events needed for automated features.
- Command Input: Information provided directly while using Enceladus commands may be processed to perform the requested action.
- Game Data: Enceladus may process and store information required for persistent game systems, including economy, inventory, exploration, crafting, pets, achievements, upgrades, daily rewards, collectibles, and related progress.
Enceladus does not intentionally request information that is unrelated to its bot features.


2. Information Stored by Enceladus
Some Enceladus features require persistent storage so that progress is preserved between bot restarts and sessions.
Depending on the features used, persistent bot data may include:
- Discord user IDs used to associate data with the correct account
- Stardust balances and bank/vault balances
- Inventory items and quantities
- Exploration charges and exploration upgrades
- Daily reward and streak information
- Pet ownership, experience, levels, and equipped pets
- Crafting progress and crafted items
- Achievement progress and unlocked rewards
- Collectible and seasonal collection progress
- Other game-related values required for Enceladus features
This information is used to operate the bot's persistent systems and is not intended to be used as a general-purpose profile of users.


3. Message Content and Reports
Enceladus does not intentionally maintain a long-term archive of ordinary Discord messages solely for logging purposes.
Message content may be processed temporarily when necessary for a command or feature. In particular, text submitted through the !qr (Quick Report) system may be forwarded to the designated server staff channel for moderation purposes.
Once information has been delivered to a Discord channel or otherwise handled by Discord, its retention and access may also be governed by Discord and the server's own moderation practices.


4. How Information Is Used
Information processed by Enceladus may be used to:
- Execute commands and provide requested bot features.
- Maintain persistent game progress and balances.
- Provide economy, inventory, exploration, crafting, pet, achievement, collectible, and seasonal systems.
- Track cooldowns, daily rewards, upgrades, and other gameplay state.
- Send automated welcome, goodbye, boost, reward, or other server messages.
- Process moderation-related reports submitted through supported commands.
- Operate bot status rotation and other internal configuration systems.
- Prevent or investigate abuse, exploitation, duplication, manipulation, or other misuse of Enceladus.
Enceladus does not intentionally sell or trade user information.


5. Discord and Third-Party Infrastructure
Enceladus operates through Discord and therefore relies on information and events provided through the Discord platform. Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply to information processed by Discord.
Enceladus may use third-party hosting, storage, networking, or other infrastructure services to operate the bot. Persistent bot databases and application files may therefore be stored on infrastructure provided by such services.
These services are used to operate Enceladus and are not intended to provide third parties with independent access to users' game data for unrelated purposes.


6. Data Retention, Backups, and Deletion
Persistent game data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Enceladus and preserve user progress.
Operational backups may also be created to protect against database corruption, accidental data loss, software updates, or other technical problems. Backups may remain available for a period of time even after active data is changed or deleted.
Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, technical requirements, backups, and the operational needs of The Cosmic Lair.
Where practical and appropriate, users may contact the server administration to request deletion or correction of persistent Enceladus data associated with their Discord account. Requests may be subject to technical limitations or legitimate operational requirements, including the handling of backups.


7. Data Security
Reasonable technical and administrative measures may be used to protect Enceladus data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.
However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through Enceladus commands unless the specific feature explicitly requires it.


8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
Enceladus may change, expand, or remove features over time. As the bot develops, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the information Enceladus accesses, stores, or processes.
The Last Updated date at the top of this document indicates when the policy was most recently revised.


9. Contact
For questions about Enceladus's data practices, persistent game data, deletion or correction requests, or this Privacy Policy, contact the administration of The Cosmic Lair through the server's designated staff or moderation channels, or contact the bot owner directly via email at stargazingdragon413@gmail.com
