Terms of Service & Privacy Policy for Enceladus
Last Updated: September 19, 2026
Enceladus is a Discord bot operated for use within The Cosmic Lair. This document explains, in general terms, what information Enceladus may access, how that information is used, how persistent game data is handled, and how third-party intellectual property and references are treated.



1. Data Collection
To provide its services, Enceladus may access information made available through the Discord API, including:
- Message Content: The bot may read message content when necessary to identify and process commands or features that require message content, including the !qr (Quick Report) system.
- Member Information: The bot may access basic Discord member information and events, such as users joining or leaving the server, display names, roles, and relevant server events used by Enceladus features.
- Command and Game Data: Enceladus may process information supplied while using bot commands, such as Stardust balances, inventory items, exploration progress, achievements, pets, upgrades, crafting progress, daily streaks, and other game-related data required for the bot to function.
- Server Events: The bot may process events such as server boosts or other relevant Discord events in order to provide automated announcements or rewards.
- Bot Configuration: Enceladus may maintain internal configuration data required for features such as status rotation, seasonal events, shop rotations, cooldowns, and other automated systems.
Enceladus only requests or processes information reasonably necessary for its features and operation.


2. Data Usage
Information accessed by Enceladus may be used for purposes including:
- Reports: Text provided through the !qr (Quick Report) command may be forwarded to the designated server staff channel for moderation and safety purposes.
- Bot Features: Game-related information is used to provide persistent features such as exploration, economy, inventory, crafting, pets, achievements, upgrades, and other Enceladus systems.
- Automation: Member and server event information may be used to trigger automated messages, announcements, rewards, or other server functionality.
- Status and Seasonal Systems: Internal data may be used to rotate the bot's status and operate temporary or seasonal features.
- Security and Moderation: Information may be processed when necessary to enforce server rules, prevent abuse, or investigate misuse of the bot.
Enceladus does not intentionally sell personal information to third parties.


3. Data Storage & Retention
Some Enceladus features require persistent storage so that game progress and server functionality continue between sessions.
Depending on the feature, stored information may include:
- Discord user IDs and related account identifiers
- Stardust and bank/vault balances
- Inventory and item quantities
- Exploration charges and upgrades
- Daily streak information
- Pet ownership, experience, and equipment
- Crafting and achievement progress
- Other data necessary to maintain Enceladus game systems
Enceladus does not intentionally maintain a long-term archive of ordinary Discord message content solely for logging purposes. Message content may be processed temporarily when required for a specific feature, such as the Quick Report system.
Persistent bot data is used for operating The Cosmic Lair and Enceladus and is not intended to be sold or traded.
Data retention may vary by feature and may be affected by technical requirements, backups, database maintenance, or other operational needs.
